About RoadRent

RoadRent is a self-drive rental service. Every car is checked before each trip, and the price you see is the price you pay.

Rental policies

  • Drivers must be 21 or older and carry a valid driving licence.
  • Rentals run from 1 to 30 days. Longer rentals cost less per day.
  • Cancel free of charge any time before the pick-up date.
  • Return the car with the same fuel level (EVs: same charge level).
